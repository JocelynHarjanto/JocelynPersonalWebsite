About Me
− □ ×

I am a passionate 2D artist with a background in Computer Science and a strong interest in the creative aspects of the game industry, particularly 2D game art, animation, and character design. I enjoy combining my technical knowledge with my passion for art to create expressive characters, engaging animations, and visually appealing experiences. I am especially drawn to cartoon and stylized art, where creativity, individuality, and personality can be expressed through distinctive visuals.

I am continuously developing my skills in 2D art, animation, and character design while exploring different styles and creative techniques. I enjoy bringing characters to life through their appearances, expressions, poses, and movements, while focusing on giving each character a unique identity. As I continue growing as an artist, I aim to refine my skills, explore new creative approaches, and develop an artistic style of my own.
